## Capsule map
- **Streaming & termination** — `references/streaming.md`: stop conditions as pure predicates over accumulated StepResults (`Promise.all(...).some()`), prepareStep per-step override-with-fallback (context mutations persist), smoothStream buffer + prefix-contract detector + fixed-delay drain with metadata carry-through, four-layer timeout ladder merged into one AbortSignal (non-semantic chunks never reset timers), pull-blocking stitchable stream where inner errors terminate everything.
