# Grammar and structure — is information hierarchical, concise, and mechanically consistent?

**Source:** Mailchimp Content Style Guide §Grammar and Mechanics, §TL;DR. **Question:** Does copy lead with the main point, use short sentences, and follow house punctuation/number rules?

## Hierarchy seam
**Path/Symbol:** paragraphs, sections, pages, emails.
**Signature:** main point first; grouped ideas; descriptive headers.
**Data Shape:** skimmable blocks for scanners and deep readers.

### Decisive pattern
```
Send your campaign
Review recipients and subject line, then schedule or send now.
```

**Flow:** group related ideas; use descriptive headers/subheaders → lead sentences, paragraphs, sections, and pages with the most important content → be concise — short words/sentences; cut unnecessary modifiers → be specific; remove vague fluff → stay consistent with guide patterns → spell out acronyms on first use unless widely known (API, HTML) → use serial (Oxford) comma → feel free to use contractions → left-align text; don’t stack bold/italic/caps/underline → read out loud when punctuation feels wrong.
**Invariant:** buried lede, wall-of-text without headings, or vague marketing fluff fails structure review.
**Probe:** inverted-pyramid check — can a skimmer get the point from first line and headings only?

## Numbers and punctuation seam
**Flow:** spell out numbers that begin sentences; otherwise use numerals (including ordinals) → commas in numbers ≥1,000 → dates: `Saturday, January 24` → time: `7 am`, `7:30 pm`, hyphenated ranges → US money: `$20`, `$19.99`; international translation copy uses `25 USD` style per i18n section → use `%` symbol → hyphen for ranges/spans and compound modifiers; em dash for asides (true em dash) → periods inside quotes; single space between sentences → easy on semicolons and exclamation points (never in failure messages).
**Invariant:** inconsistent number/date/time formatting within same doc fails consistency review.
**Probe:** grep dates/times/currency against tables in learning note.

## Verdict
Lead with main point, concise specific prose, Oxford comma, consistent numbers/punctuation. Learning note: `mailchimp-style-learning-note.md`.
