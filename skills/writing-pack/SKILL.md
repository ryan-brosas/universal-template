# Writing pack

Choose the matching writing outcome, not every stylistic guide. Resolve each
procedure's paths from its own directory.

- Case-study, customer-story, success-story or portfolio proof pages:
  [case-study-pages](../../knowledge/playbooks/case-study-pages/README.md). This
  specific procedure takes precedence over general page copywriting.
- Other conversion-focused copy, positioning, headlines, CTAs or onboarding
  text: [copywriting](../../knowledge/playbooks/copywriting/README.md).
- Polish prose in the house style:
  [house-writing-style](../../knowledge/playbooks/house-writing-style/README.md).
- Technical documentation and architecture decisions:
  [documentation-and-adrs](../../knowledge/playbooks/documentation-and-adrs/README.md).
- Markdown conventions:
  [markdown-writing-practices](../../knowledge/playbooks/markdown-writing-practices/README.md).

For developer-documentation conventions, code examples, Mailchimp content or
Notion workflows, select additional [writing topics](references/topics.md) only for
distinct active outcomes. Keep facts and claims grounded in the user's source; do
not create extra documents merely because a procedure exists. Private proposal
procedures remain local and are not copied into this public pack.
