# Voice and tone — does copy sound plainspoken, human, and appropriately toned?

**Source:** Mailchimp Content Style Guide §Voice and Tone, §TL;DR. **Question:** Is clarity prioritized over entertainment with active, positive, jargon-free language matched to reader state?

## Voice seam
**Path/Symbol:** user-facing strings, help, marketing, support replies.
**Signature:** human, familiar, straightforward; dry humor optional; active voice.
**Data Shape:** educate without patronizing; demystify B2B terms.

### Decisive pattern
```
We help you send your first campaign in minutes—not after a sales call.
```

**Flow:** write like a compassionate business partner — plainspoken, not hype → value clarity over fluffy metaphors, upsells, and cheap emotional plays → be genuine and warm; translate jargon into plain English → humor is dry and subtle when natural — skip forced jokes → adjust **tone** to reader context (confused support vs celebratory success) while **voice** stays consistent → use active voice by default; passive only when emphasizing the acted-upon subject → avoid slang and undefined jargon → prefer positive phrasing (“Do X to get Y”) over negative (“You can’t Y unless X”) → contractions are fine for friendly tone → read aloud when unsure.
**Invariant:** hype-heavy, patronizing, or passive-by-default copy fails Mailchimp voice review.
**Probe:** highlight passive constructions (`was`, `by`); scan for unexplained acronyms; check failure/alert strings for inappropriate humor or exclamation marks.

## Principles seam
**Flow:** every piece should be clear, useful, friendly, and appropriate to context → prioritize helping users finish work over entertaining → do not write in mascot/brand character voice (Freddie does not speak).
**Invariant:** off-brand forced humor in error states or legal-sensitive flows fails appropriateness check.
**Probe:** classify content type (UI error, blog, help) against tone expectations.

## Verdict
Plainspoken active voice, context-aware tone, positive framing, jargon defined. Learning note: `mailchimp-style-learning-note.md`.
