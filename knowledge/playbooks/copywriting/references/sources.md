seven categories. The study sampled the full title/category inventory, then read
foundational and specialized articles across 2012-2025. Newer 2026 entries were
inventoried but not treated as established guidance when their publication date
was in the future relative to the environment.

This ledger records provenance, not copied article content.

## Source tensions retained

- Clarity is the entry gate; grounded personality may still outperform a clear
  control. Treat creativity as a testable treatment, not a rule.
- Page and sequence length follows the beliefs and state changes required, not a
  universal word or email count.
- Social proof helps when trust or uncertainty is the barrier; it is not
  mandatory decoration.
- Bundled redesign wins validate the bundle in that context, not each tactic.
- Opens, clicks, and plan selections diagnose local behavior and do not by
  themselves prove purchase, retention, or revenue.
