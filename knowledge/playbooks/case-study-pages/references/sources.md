# Sources and attribution

## Primary source

This playbook is a procedural adaptation of:

- Joel Klettke, [“I've Helped Create 150+ Case Studies. Here's (Almost)
  Everything I've Learned.”](https://copyhackers.com/2019/05/writing-case-studies/),
  Copyhackers, published May 2, 2019 and updated August 24, 2020.

The adaptation retains the source's strategy-first selection, customer interview,
story structure, context-led length, and reuse concepts while recasting them for
website case-study and portfolio pages. It does not carry the article's marketing
statistics forward as operating facts.

This is a synthesis, not a reproduction of the article. The original wording,
examples, images, and other publisher materials remain at the source; consult the
publisher before reusing them.

## Local integration

The evidence gates, claim qualification, page handoffs, and placeholders align
with this repository's [copywriting playbook](../../copywriting/README.md) so the
case-study procedure does not create a second source of truth for general
conversion-page practice.
