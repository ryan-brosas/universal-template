Use this session's experience to improve future work. Follow the experience-to-
adoption procedure in knowledge/playbooks/session-improvement-compiler/README.md.
Start with what actually happened, not a search for skills to edit. Study useful
successes, failures, corrections, shortcuts, and verification; distinguish evidence
from plausible explanations and project-specific boundaries from reusable methods.

Decide what improvement each worthwhile lesson calls for, then find its natural
owner: code, configuration, tests, a project decision, a prompt, a reusable method,
or nothing permanent. Improve an existing owner where it fits. Work within the
authorized scope; name concrete blockers and continue with authorized improvements.
Verify the affected behavior or future task, not just the written artifact. Explain
what was saved, adopted, or shown useful without claiming more than the evidence.
Do not create a session summary, memory system, or mandatory report.
